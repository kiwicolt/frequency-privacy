Privacy Policy for Frequency

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Frequency ("the app") is an IPTV player for Android TV. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: Frequency does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data to us or to any third party. The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Information you provide

To use Frequency you add one or more playlists — either an Xtream Codes login (server URL, username, password) or an M3U/XMLTV URL, and optionally a custom EPG URL. This information is stored only on your device so the app can connect to the service you chose. It is never sent to the developer and is never shared with anyone other than the IPTV provider whose address you entered.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following locally on your device, using Android's on-device storage. None of it leaves your device except as described under "Network connections":

  • Your playlists and their credentials/URLs.
  • Your preferences — theme, favourites, hidden channels, sort order, last channel.
  • Cached channel lists, program-guide (EPG) data, and images, to speed up loading.
  • Any recordings you create, saved to a storage location you select.

You can remove this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Network connections

Frequency connects directly to the servers you configure in order to load playlists, program guides, and video streams. These connections go to your IPTV provider, not to the developer. The app may connect over HTTP or HTTPS depending on what your provider supports. Your use of any third-party IPTV service is governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy; the developer has no control over and no visibility into those services.

If you use the local-network scan feature, the app looks for devices on your own local network to help you add a source. This activity stays on your local network.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs to function:

  • Internet — to load streams, playlists, and guide data.
  • Wake lock / foreground service — to keep playback and recording running while in use.
  • Notifications — to show recording status.

Children's privacy

Frequency is a general-audience media player and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Content disclaimer

Frequency is a neutral media player. It does not host, provide, bundle, or supply any channels, streams, or media content. All content is supplied by the playlists and services that you, the user, choose to add. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to access any content you play through the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
